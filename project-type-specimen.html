Clarendon Type Specimen

Graphic Design · October 2025

A set of three postcards that explore the Clarendon typeface — its history, structure, and real-world uses — using a playful design inspired by children's wooden blocks.

Role Graphic Designer
Type Print Design · Solo
Tools
InDesign Photoshop

Overview

A bold slab-serif with a rich history

Originally designed in the 19th century, Clarendon is a bold slab-serif that balances strength and warmth. This project highlights its versatility through a block-inspired design system.

Concept

Bright colors, playful structure

A playful, block-inspired postcard series that communicates typography fundamentals through composition and color.

Three Postcards

Card 01

History

Introduction and historical context — where Clarendon came from and why it endures.

Card 02

Anatomy

A breakdown of type anatomy and the environments where Clarendon appears in the wild.

Card 03

Comparison

A full type specimen alongside a direct comparison with Helvetica — contrasting character.
