Evently

Mobile App · January–April 2025

Evently is a mobile app that helps college students discover campus events using a swipe-based interface similar to Tinder.

Role Developer & Designer
Type Team Project
Tools
React Node.js Figma

The Problem

Students miss out on campus life

College students often struggle to discover events that fit their interests or schedule, missing opportunities to engage with clubs, social activities, and workshops.

The Solution

Swipe to discover what's happening

A Tinder-style swipe interface for campus events, curating a personalized feed based on each student's interests with quick RSVP, calendar integration, and in-app chat.

UXR Takeaways

01

Familiar interaction patterns reduce learning curve

Leveraging well-known gestures like swiping meant users needed almost no onboarding to start discovering events.

02

Micro-interactions increase engagement

Small animated responses to swipes and saves made the experience feel rewarding and encouraged continued use.

03

Community-building features strengthen retention

In-app chat and shared calendar events gave students a reason to return even after finding an event they liked.

03

Add events to your calendar and coordinate with other attendees via in-app chat.
