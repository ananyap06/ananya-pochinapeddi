Greeting Cards

Graphic Design · 2020–Present

A long-running personal project creating custom greeting cards using physical materials, layered paper, and digital refinement.

Role Graphic Designer
Type Print & Physical Design
Tools
Cricut Design Space Photoshop Procreate

Problem

How can physical materials, typography, and layering be used to create meaningful, emotional design?

  • Physical format limits space and layout flexibility
  • Layering requires careful planning for depth and readability
  • Presentation matters just as much as fabrication

Solution

Design and fabricate custom greeting cards that combine thoughtful typography, dimensional layering, and carefully staged photography to create emotionally engaging physical artifacts that are personalized and tailored to each individual recipient.
