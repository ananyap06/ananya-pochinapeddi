MBTA Flow

App Redesign · February 2025

A redesign of the MBTA mobile app focused on clarity, accessibility, and improved navigation.

Role Designer
Type Solo Project
Context Design Processes & Practices
Tools
Figma Photoshop

The Problem

A cluttered, unintuitive transit app

The existing MBTA app often feels cluttered and inconsistent. New riders and students struggle to plan trips efficiently, find live updates, or locate key features like ticketing and accessibility settings.

The Solution

Simplified, cohesive, accessible

A redesigned app with a clearer navigation system, better-surfaced real-time information, and improved accessibility options, all while maintaining the familiar MBTA identity.

Key Insights

What wasn't working

01

Hidden ticketing

Buried under confusing multi-level navigation.

02

Cluttered maps

No visual hierarchy — hard to read on the move.

03

Poor accessibility

Key settings buried several levels deep.

UXR Takeaways

What research revealed

01

Speed over visual complexity

Transit apps must prioritize fast, scannable information.

02

Clear hierarchy reduces cognitive load

Faster decisions when structure is obvious.

03

Accessibility should be primary

Not an afterthought buried in settings.
